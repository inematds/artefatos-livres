How it works

Six pieces, no jargon

🔑 Link with a key

Like the key to a room: whoever has the link gets in, whoever doesn't gets "private page". Want to remove someone? Ask your AI to change the key and send the new link.

📚 New version, same link

Like a notebook with numbered pages: the link always shows the latest, and each earlier version stays saved under its number.

📲 Install as an app

In Chrome or Edge, "install" appears when the page is on a secure address (https). It gets an icon and its own window, like any program, and opens even without internet with the last version seen.

🗂️ Team data

Like a board on the wall of the room: what one person writes, the others see within seconds. It lives in a file on your computer, not in someone else's cloud.

💬 Comments

A button in the corner of every page. The team leaves the note there, with name and time, and you take the note to whichever AI you like.

🧳 Take everything with you

One command gathers the page, the versions and the data (as a spreadsheet and as text) into a folder of yours. The access key doesn't go with it.

🤖 And the AI inside the page?

Optional and off at first. If you turn it on, the page can ask questions to the AI that you choose (Claude, Codex or a model on your PC), on your account, with a limit of questions per person.

✅ What the kit does

  • page by link, private by default
  • versions on the same link
  • install as an app (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android)
  • shared lists and sign-ups, comments
  • export everything as a spreadsheet

🚫 What it doesn't promise

  • connecting to Gmail, Slack or the company database (Claude's "connectors")
  • tagging the AI in a comment and having it fix things on its own
  • per-person login and roles (the boss sees everything, the student sees only their own)
  • ready-made templates for documents, slides and spreadsheets

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to program?

No. You ask your AI with the text from "Getting started". To change the page, you just ask too.

Does it cost anything?

The kit is free and open. On your computer, zero cost, but only people on the same network can open it. For everyone to open it from anywhere, you need a server connected to the internet: a rented computer that stays on all the time (the smallest cost a few dollars a month) or a mini PC of your own. For "install as app" to appear, it needs an https address; your AI sets that up.

So do I drop Claude?

No need. Use Claude, Codex or another AI to write the pages. What changes is where the page and the data live: with you.

Is it OK for sensitive data?

Whoever has the link gets in and edits. For salaries, health or customer data, use a system with per-person login. The kit is for everyday "small software".

Does it work on a phone?

It opens on any phone. Installing as an app works on Android through Chrome, with the page on a secure address (https); on iPhone, use Safari's "Add to Home Screen".

What if I already have artifacts in Claude?

An artifact's content is an ordinary page. Ask the AI to bring it into the kit following the repository's contract; you export the data from Claude and ask the AI to load it into the new page.
